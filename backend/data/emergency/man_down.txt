Document: General Man Down and Medical Emergency Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO
Medical Protocol: NO

Scope:
General first-response safety guidance for an injured, collapsed,
unresponsive, or otherwise potentially seriously ill person in an
industrial environment.

Keywords:
man down, worker down, unconscious, unresponsive, injured, injury,
collapse, collapsed, bleeding, medical emergency, casualty,
first aid, cpr, rescue

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Immediately notify the site's designated emergency or medical response
service according to the applicable site procedure.

Before approaching the casualty, consider whether hazards such as
electricity, fire, smoke, toxic gas, chemicals, moving machinery,
traffic, work at height, confined spaces, or structural hazards may
make the area unsafe.

Do not enter an unsafe or uncontrolled hazardous area to reach a
casualty.

If the area is safe, provide assistance only within the limits of your
training and according to the applicable site emergency or medical
procedure.

First Aid:
First aid and CPR should be provided by appropriately trained personnel
according to their current training and the applicable site procedure.

This general guidance does not provide medical diagnosis, CPR
instructions, medication instructions, or injury-specific treatment
steps.

Casualty Movement:
Do not unnecessarily move an injured or collapsed person.

Movement should only occur when required by an immediate danger or
when directed by appropriately trained or authorized responders,
according to the applicable emergency procedure.

Information for Responders:
Provide responders with available information about:

- the incident
- the casualty's location
- known or suspected hazards
- observed events or conditions
- relevant exposure information if known

Do not place yourself at additional risk while obtaining this
information.

Avoid:
Do not enter an unsafe area to perform an unauthorized rescue.

Do not perform medical procedures beyond your training.

Do not provide medication or medical treatment based solely on this
general guidance.

Do not unnecessarily move the casualty.

Do not expose additional personnel to electrical, fire, gas, chemical,
machinery, confined-space, height, or other hazards while attempting
to help.

Escalation:
Notify the designated emergency or medical response service according
to the applicable site procedure.

Qualified medical and emergency responders should take over as soon
as available.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- medical diagnosis
- detailed CPR instructions
- medication instructions
- injury-specific treatment
- plant-specific emergency phone numbers
- ambulance contact details
- rescue entry procedures
- electrical rescue procedures
- confined-space rescue procedures
- chemical-specific medical treatment

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC medical procedure, first-aid protocol,
or plant-specific emergency response procedure.

Applicable site emergency procedures, current first-aid training,
medical protocols, and instructions from qualified emergency or
medical responders always take priority.
