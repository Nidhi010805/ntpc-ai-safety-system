Document: General Emergency Evacuation Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General safety guidance for responding to an evacuation alarm or
authorized evacuation instruction in an industrial environment.

Keywords:
evacuation, evacuate, emergency, alarm, evacuation alarm,
emergency exit, exit, assembly point, muster point, escape route,
emergency route, re-entry, personnel accountability

Emergency Evacuation:
When an evacuation alarm or authorized evacuation instruction is
issued, follow the applicable site emergency procedure.

Stop work when safe to do so and evacuate according to the applicable
site procedure.

Use only evacuation routes designated as safe by the applicable site
emergency plan or authorized emergency personnel.

Follow instructions from authorized emergency personnel.

Keep away from hazardous, restricted, smoke-filled, contaminated,
or otherwise unsafe areas during evacuation.

Assembly and Accountability:
After evacuation, proceed according to the site's approved assembly
or muster arrangements.

Follow the applicable personnel accountability procedure.

Provide requested information to authorized emergency personnel.

If someone is believed to be missing, trapped, or still inside the
affected area, report this information immediately to authorized
emergency responders.

Do not attempt an unauthorized rescue.

Avoid:
Do not enter a hazardous area during evacuation.

Do not return to collect personal belongings.

Do not use an evacuation route that has been declared unsafe or
restricted.

Do not interfere with emergency response operations.

Do not re-enter the affected area until authorized personnel permit
re-entry.

Escalation:
Follow applicable alarms and instructions from authorized emergency
personnel.

Report missing, trapped, injured, or potentially exposed personnel
to the designated emergency response personnel according to the
applicable site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- plant-specific evacuation routes
- emergency exit locations
- assembly or muster point locations
- evacuation maps
- emergency phone numbers
- area-specific escape directions
- head-count or accountability arrangements for a specific plant
- emergency transport arrangements

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC emergency plan and is not a plant-specific
evacuation procedure.

Actual evacuation routes, exits, assembly points, accountability
arrangements, and emergency instructions must come from the applicable
plant-approved emergency plan and authorized emergency personnel.
