Document: General Work at Height Safety Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General safety guidance for work where a person could fall and
sustain injury.

Keywords:
height, work at height, fall, falling, harness, ladder, scaffold,
scaffolding, roof, platform, guardrail, lifeline, anchorage,
fall protection, elevated work

Hazard Recognition:
Work at height may expose personnel to falls, falling objects,
unstable access equipment, unsuitable working platforms, or other
serious hazards.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Work at height should be properly planned, risk assessed, supervised,
and performed by competent personnel according to the applicable
site procedure.

Use suitable working platforms, guardrails, scaffolds, ladders, or
fall-protection systems according to the task, hazard assessment,
and applicable approved procedure.

Inspect required fall-protection and access equipment before use
according to applicable requirements.

Where required by the applicable hazard assessment or procedure,
use an approved full-body harness and compatible fall-protection
system.

Control the area below height work when falling objects may create
a hazard.

Weather and environmental conditions should be considered before
and during work at height according to the applicable procedure.

Unsafe Condition:
If required fall protection is missing, damaged, unsuitable, or
incorrectly used, the affected work should not begin or continue
until the applicable safety requirements are satisfied.

Report unsafe ladders, scaffolds, platforms, guardrails, harnesses,
connectors, lifelines, or other fall-protection equipment according
to the applicable site procedure.

Avoid:
Do not use visibly damaged ladders, scaffolds, harnesses, connectors,
lifelines, or working platforms.

Do not improvise unsafe working platforms or access arrangements.

Do not intentionally bypass required fall-protection controls.

Do not perform work at height without required authorization,
planning, supervision, or safety controls.

Do not select an anchorage point, lifeline configuration, or
fall-arrest arrangement solely from this general guidance.

Escalation:
Missing or damaged fall protection, unsafe access equipment,
uncontrolled falling-object hazards, or other unsafe height-work
conditions should be reported to the appropriate supervisor or
safety personnel according to the applicable site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- plant-specific work-at-height permit requirements
- minimum height thresholds for a specific site
- anchorage point selection
- anchorage strength calculations
- lifeline design
- fall-clearance calculations
- scaffold erection instructions
- ladder setup dimensions
- rescue-from-height procedures
- plant-specific PPE requirements

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP and is not a plant-specific
work-at-height procedure.

Applicable permit-to-work requirements, hazard assessments,
work-at-height procedures, equipment instructions, and directions
from authorized personnel always take priority.
