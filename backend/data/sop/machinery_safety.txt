Document: General Machinery Safety Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General safety guidance for machinery, conveyors, motors, rotating
equipment, moving parts, and machinery-related hazards in an
industrial environment.

Keywords:
machine, machinery, rotating, conveyor, motor, guard, guarding,
moving parts, pinch point, crushing, cutting, maintenance, jam,
isolation, lockout, tagout, loto, vibration, overheating

Hazard Recognition:
Exposed moving, rotating, cutting, crushing, drawing-in, and
pinch-point hazards may cause serious injury.

Abnormal noise, vibration, overheating, leakage, damaged guarding,
or unexpected equipment movement may indicate an unsafe condition.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Keep personnel away from exposed moving or rotating machinery and
other hazardous moving parts.

Report abnormal noise, vibration, overheating, leakage, damaged
guarding, or other suspected machinery hazards according to the
applicable site procedure.

Follow applicable warning signs, barricades, operating restrictions,
and instructions from authorized personnel.

Do not operate machinery when required safety guards or protective
devices are removed, damaged, bypassed, or defeated.

Moving Machinery:
Do not reach into moving machinery.

Do not attempt to clear a jam while machinery is moving.

Loose clothing, jewelry, hair, and other items that could become
entangled should be controlled according to applicable safe working
requirements.

Maintenance and Isolation:
Before maintenance, inspection, clearing a jam, or another
intervention requiring hazardous energy control, equipment should be
isolated according to the applicable approved energy-isolation
procedure.

Lockout/tagout or other required isolation controls should only be
applied or removed by trained and authorized personnel according to
the applicable approved procedure.

This general guidance does not provide equipment-specific shutdown,
isolation, lockout/tagout, restart, or testing steps.

Avoid:
Do not bypass or defeat machinery guards or protective devices.

Do not reach through or around guarding into hazardous moving parts.

Do not perform maintenance or intervention on hazardous machinery
without the controls required by the applicable approved procedure.

Do not operate switches, breakers, isolation devices, or machinery
based solely on this general guidance.

Do not restart equipment unless permitted by the applicable approved
procedure and authorized personnel.

Escalation:
Suspected unsafe machinery conditions should be reported to the
appropriate supervisor, control room, maintenance authority, safety
personnel, or other designated personnel according to the applicable
site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- equipment-specific shutdown sequences
- machinery restart sequences
- plant-specific isolation points
- lockout/tagout steps for specific equipment
- stored-energy release procedures
- equipment settings
- guard adjustment procedures
- maintenance or repair instructions

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP and is not an equipment-specific
operating or maintenance procedure.

Equipment manuals, applicable plant-approved operating procedures,
energy-isolation procedures, maintenance procedures, and instructions
from authorized personnel always take priority.
