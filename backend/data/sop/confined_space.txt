Document: General Confined Space Safety Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General safety guidance for recognizing confined-space hazards and
responding to unsafe confined-space conditions.

Keywords:
confined space, tank, vessel, pit, chamber, oxygen, toxic gas,
atmosphere, atmospheric testing, ventilation, entry permit,
standby person, rescue

Hazard Recognition:
Confined spaces may contain hazardous atmospheres, oxygen deficiency
or enrichment, toxic gases, physical hazards, or other conditions
that may become dangerous even when the space initially appears safe.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Do not enter a confined space without required authorization and the
applicable approved entry procedure.

A confined-space entry may require hazard assessment, isolation,
atmospheric testing, ventilation, communication, standby personnel,
and rescue arrangements according to the applicable site procedure.

Required atmospheric testing should be performed using approved
equipment by competent and authorized personnel according to the
applicable procedure.

Maintain required communication between entrants and designated
standby personnel.

If unsafe conditions develop, entrants should leave the space
according to the applicable approved procedure and instructions from
authorized personnel.

Notify the designated supervisor, safety personnel, control room, or
emergency response personnel when required by the applicable site
procedure.

Rescue:
Do not enter a confined space to rescue another person unless you are
part of the authorized rescue response and the required controls and
rescue arrangements are in place.

An unplanned rescue attempt may expose additional personnel to the
same hazard.

Avoid:
Do not enter without required authorization.

Do not assume that a confined space is safe because it appears normal.

Do not perform atmospheric testing, isolation, ventilation setup, or
other specialized entry controls unless trained, competent, authorized,
and permitted under the applicable procedure.

Do not attempt an unauthorized confined-space rescue.

Do not bypass entry permits, monitoring requirements, standby
arrangements, or other required controls.

Escalation:
Unsafe conditions, loss of communication, suspected hazardous
atmosphere, collapse of an entrant, or another confined-space
emergency should be escalated according to the applicable site
emergency and rescue procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- plant-specific confined-space locations
- permit approval requirements for a specific plant
- acceptable atmospheric limits
- gas detector settings
- equipment isolation points
- ventilation calculations
- rescue entry steps
- rescue equipment configuration
- plant-specific PPE requirements
- emergency phone numbers

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP and is not a plant-specific
confined-space entry or rescue procedure.

Applicable site-approved confined-space entry, isolation, monitoring,
rescue, and emergency procedures always take priority.
