Document: General PPE Safety Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General guidance for the use and condition of Personal Protective
Equipment in an industrial environment.

Keywords:
ppe, personal protective equipment, helmet, hard hat, gloves,
goggles, safety glasses, safety shoes, safety footwear, face shield,
hearing protection, harness, respirator, respiratory protection,
damaged ppe, missing ppe

General Safety Guidance:
Required PPE should be selected according to the task, hazard
assessment, permit requirements, and applicable site procedure.

Personnel should inspect required PPE before use according to
applicable instructions and site requirements.

Do not begin or continue a task when mandatory PPE is missing,
damaged, unsuitable, or incorrectly worn.

Damaged or defective PPE should be removed from service and reported
according to the applicable site procedure.

Safety helmets, eye protection, gloves, safety footwear, hearing
protection, respiratory protection, fall-protection equipment, and
other PPE should be used where required by the applicable hazard
assessment or site procedure.

Specialized PPE should only be used by personnel who are trained or
otherwise competent in its correct use and limitations when such
training or competency is required.

PPE Limitations:
PPE does not eliminate the underlying hazard.

PPE does not replace hazard elimination, isolation, engineering
controls, permits, safe systems of work, or other required safety
controls.

Avoid:
Do not use damaged or defective PPE.

Do not intentionally bypass mandatory PPE requirements.

Do not assume that one type of PPE is suitable for every hazard.

Do not select specialized respiratory, electrical, chemical, or
fall-protection PPE solely from this general guidance.

Do not continue a task when required PPE is unavailable or unsuitable.

Escalation:
Missing, damaged, unsuitable, or uncertain PPE requirements should be
reported to the appropriate supervisor or safety personnel according
to the applicable site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- plant-specific PPE matrices
- task-specific PPE combinations
- voltage-rated PPE requirements
- chemical-specific PPE selection
- respirator cartridge selection
- respirator fit-testing requirements
- fall-protection equipment configuration
- equipment-specific PPE requirements

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP and is not a plant-specific PPE
procedure.

Applicable task-specific hazard assessments, permits, manufacturer
instructions, and plant-approved PPE requirements always take
priority.
