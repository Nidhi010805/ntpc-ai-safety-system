Document: General Fire Safety Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General guidance for recognizing and responding to a possible
industrial fire or significant unexplained smoke.

Keywords:
fire, aag, smoke, dhua, dhuan, burning, flame, fire alarm,
transformer smoke, electrical fire, boiler fire

Hazard Recognition:
Visible flames, uncontrolled burning, significant unexplained
smoke, or signs of fire should be treated as a potential
emergency.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Raise or confirm the site's emergency alarm according to the
applicable site procedure.

Notify the designated control room or emergency response team
according to site procedure.

Warn nearby personnel when it is safe to do so.

Keep personnel away from the affected or potentially hazardous
area.

Follow the site's approved evacuation procedure and instructions
from authorized emergency personnel.

After evacuation, proceed according to the site's approved
assembly and personnel accountability procedure.

Firefighting:
Firefighting equipment should only be used by personnel who are
trained, authorized, permitted by site procedure, and able to
maintain a safe escape route.

Do not instruct an untrained or unauthorized person to fight
a fire.

Avoid:
Do not enter or re-enter a smoke-filled or fire-affected area.

Do not approach burning or potentially energized equipment
unless authorized and permitted by the applicable site procedure.

Do not take unnecessary personal risk to protect equipment or
property.

Do not provide equipment-specific shutdown, isolation, switching,
or firefighting instructions from this general guidance.

Escalation:
For a possible or active fire emergency, notify the designated
control room or emergency response team according to the
applicable site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide plant-specific alarm locations,
emergency phone numbers, evacuation routes, assembly points,
equipment isolation points, electrical switching sequences,
fire-extinguisher selection instructions, or equipment-specific
firefighting procedures.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP and is not a plant-specific
emergency procedure.

The applicable plant-approved fire and emergency procedures and
instructions from authorized personnel always take priority.
