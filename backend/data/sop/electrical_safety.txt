Document: General Electrical Safety Guidance
Document Type: General Industrial Safety Guidance
Plant Specific: NO
Official NTPC SOP: NO

Scope:
General safety guidance for recognizing and responding to possible
electrical hazards in an industrial environment.

Keywords:
electrical, electric, electricity, shock, electric shock, spark,
sparks, arc, arc flash, switchyard, transformer, electrical panel,
cable, breaker, isolator, isolation, energized equipment, voltage,
smoke, burning, short circuit

Hazard Recognition:
Smoke, sparks, burning smell, abnormal arcing, exposed electrical
parts, damaged electrical equipment, or other signs of an electrical
fault should be treated as a potentially serious hazard.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Keep personnel away from affected or potentially hazardous electrical
equipment.

Maintain a safe distance from the hazardous area according to the
applicable site procedure and instructions from authorized personnel.

Notify the designated control room, supervisor, authorized electrical
personnel, or emergency response team according to the applicable
site procedure.

Follow applicable site alarms, barricades, warning signs, evacuation
instructions, and directions from authorized personnel.

If smoke or fire is present, treat the situation as a possible
electrical and fire emergency and follow the applicable site emergency
procedure.

Electrical Work and Isolation:
Electrical isolation, switching, lockout/tagout, testing for absence
of voltage, grounding, or other electrical work must only be performed
by trained and authorized personnel following the applicable approved
procedure.

This general guidance does not provide equipment-specific switching,
shutdown, isolation, lockout/tagout, testing, or grounding steps.

Avoid:
Do not touch exposed conductors, damaged cables, electrical panels,
transformers, or other potentially energized equipment when an
electrical fault is suspected.

Do not enter a restricted electrical hazard area unless trained,
authorized, and permitted under the applicable site procedure.

Do not operate a breaker, isolator, switch, transformer, or other
electrical equipment based only on this general guidance.

Do not attempt electrical isolation, switching, lockout/tagout,
voltage testing, grounding, repair, or fault investigation unless
trained, authorized, and permitted under the applicable approved
procedure.

Do not use firefighting equipment on or near electrical equipment
unless trained, authorized, the equipment is suitable for the hazard,
and its use is permitted by the applicable site procedure.

Do not re-enter a restricted or hazardous electrical area until
authorized personnel permit re-entry.

Escalation:
Notify the designated control room, supervisor, authorized electrical
personnel, or emergency response team according to the applicable
site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- plant-specific electrical isolation points
- transformer switching sequences
- breaker or isolator operating sequences
- equipment shutdown sequences
- plant-specific lockout/tagout steps
- voltage-specific approach distances
- plant-specific PPE requirements
- emergency phone numbers
- equipment or protection settings
- relay settings
- electrical test procedures

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP and is not a plant-specific electrical
operating or emergency procedure.

Applicable plant-approved electrical safety procedures, operating
procedures, emergency procedures, and instructions from authorized
personnel always take priority.
