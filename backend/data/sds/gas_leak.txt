Scope:
General safety guidance for recognizing and responding to a suspected
toxic, flammable, or unknown gas release in an industrial environment.

Keywords:
gas, gas leak, toxic gas, flammable gas, chlorine, ammonia, fumes,
vapor, vapour, smell, gas detector, detector alarm, gas alarm,
release, leakage

Hazard Recognition:
A suspected toxic, flammable, or unknown gas release should be treated
as a potential emergency.

Gas detector alarms, unusual fumes, or other indications of a possible
gas release should be treated according to the applicable site
emergency procedure.

Do not rely on smell to determine whether an area is safe.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Notify the designated control room or emergency response team
according to the applicable site procedure.

Keep unauthorized and unprotected personnel away from the suspected
release area.

Follow applicable gas detection alarms and the site's approved
evacuation or shelter instructions.

Follow instructions from authorized emergency personnel.

Do not deliberately approach the suspected release to identify the
gas by smell.

Gas Leak Intervention:
Do not attempt to locate, stop, isolate, repair, or otherwise intervene
in a hazardous gas release unless trained, authorized, properly
equipped, and permitted by the applicable approved procedure.

Respiratory Protection:
Respiratory protective equipment should only be selected and used
according to the applicable hazard assessment, respiratory-protection
program, chemical information, and site procedure.

Specialized respiratory protective equipment should only be used by
appropriately trained and authorized personnel.

Avoid:
Do not enter or deliberately approach a suspected hazardous gas
release area without the required authorization and protection.

Do not attempt to identify an unknown gas by smelling it.

Do not attempt to stop, isolate, or repair a hazardous gas leak based
only on this general guidance.

Do not assume that an area is safe simply because no smell is present.

Do not select respiratory protective equipment solely from this
general guidance.

Escalation:
A suspected hazardous gas release should be reported to the designated
control room or emergency response team according to the applicable
site procedure.

Follow the site's applicable evacuation, shelter, emergency response,
and re-entry instructions.

Chemical Information:
When the released substance is known, the applicable chemical-specific
Safety Data Sheet and approved emergency procedure should be consulted.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- chemical-specific exposure limits
- gas detector alarm settings
- safe concentration limits
- plant-specific evacuation routes
- plant-specific shelter locations
- emergency phone numbers
- valve or isolation locations
- leak isolation steps
- repair procedures
- respiratory equipment selection
- respirator cartridge selection
- plant-specific PPE requirements
- chemical-specific first-aid instructions

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP, plant-specific gas-release procedure,
or chemical-specific Safety Data Sheet.

The applicable chemical-specific SDS, site-approved gas-release
emergency procedure, and instructions from authorized emergency
personnel always take priority.
