Scope:
General safety guidance for recognizing and responding to a suspected
hazardous chemical spill or liquid chemical release in an industrial
environment.

Keywords:
chemical, chemical spill, spill, liquid leak, leakage, hazardous
chemical, acid, caustic, toxic, corrosive, contamination, chemical
exposure, chemical release, sds, safety data sheet

Hazard Recognition:
An unknown chemical spill or release should be treated as potentially
hazardous.

A chemical release may present toxic, corrosive, flammable, reactive,
or other hazards depending on the substance involved.

Do not assume that an unknown chemical is safe based on its appearance,
smell, or quantity.

Immediate Safety Guidance:
Keep unauthorized and unprotected personnel away from the affected
area.

Notify the designated control room, supervisor, safety personnel, or
emergency response team according to the applicable site procedure.

Follow applicable alarms, barricades, evacuation instructions, and
directions from authorized emergency personnel.

When the substance is known, consult the applicable chemical-specific
Safety Data Sheet and approved site procedure before selecting response
measures.

Avoid:
Do not touch an unknown or hazardous chemical directly.

Do not deliberately smell or taste a chemical to identify it.

Do not walk through a chemical spill or contaminated area.

Do not attempt to identify an unknown chemical through direct exposure.

Do not attempt to contain, neutralize, clean, transfer, or stop a
hazardous chemical release unless trained, authorized, properly
equipped, and permitted by the applicable approved procedure.

Avoid actions that may spread contamination.

PPE and Spill Response:
Chemical-response PPE should be selected according to the identified
chemical, applicable Safety Data Sheet, hazard assessment, and approved
site procedure.

Do not select chemical-response PPE solely from this general guidance.

Spill containment, cleanup, neutralization, or other direct response
should only be performed by appropriately trained and authorized
personnel using the controls required by the applicable procedure.

Exposure:
If chemical exposure is suspected or has occurred, follow the
applicable chemical-specific Safety Data Sheet and approved site
emergency or medical procedure.

Report the exposure according to the applicable site procedure.

Escalation:
A suspected hazardous chemical release should be reported to the
designated control room, supervisor, safety personnel, or emergency
response team according to the applicable site procedure.

Limitations:
This document does not provide:

- chemical-specific exposure limits
- chemical identification procedures
- neutralization instructions
- spill containment methods for a specific chemical
- cleanup quantities or materials
- valve or equipment isolation locations
- chemical-specific PPE selection
- respirator or cartridge selection
- chemical-specific first-aid instructions
- plant-specific evacuation routes
- emergency phone numbers

If this information is requested, the available general safety
guidance does not provide enough information for that action.

Important:
This is general industrial safety guidance.

It is not an official NTPC SOP, plant-specific chemical emergency
procedure, or chemical-specific Safety Data Sheet.

The applicable chemical-specific SDS, plant-approved emergency
procedure, and instructions from authorized personnel always take
priority.
